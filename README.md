### Hi there 👋 I'm Apri Junaidi

I am a **Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science** at the Faculty of Computing, **Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM)**, Johor Bahru, where I also work as a **Research Assistant**. My research is about computer vision for agriculture: building **efficient deep-learning detectors for plant disease** that can run on **resource-constrained edge devices** in the field.

---

### 🔬 Research interests

- **Agricultural object detection**: detecting plant (rice) leaf diseases in real field images
- **Attention mechanisms**: parameter-efficient and **parameter-free attention** for lightweight detectors
- **Edge computing**: real-time crop monitoring on embedded hardware such as the NVIDIA Jetson Nano
- **Evaluation rigour**: reproducible benchmarking, multi-seed experiments and bootstrap confidence intervals
- **Precision agriculture** and field image datasets

### 📚 Publications and projects

- 📄 A systematic literature review on deep learning for plant disease detection, published in **IEEE Access** (2025)
- 🧰 [**ai-assisted-slr-framework**](https://github.com/aprijunaidi/ai-assisted-slr-framework): a workflow for AI-assisted systematic literature reviews with Zotero and Rayyan
- 🌾 Further work on lightweight attention, on-device rice disease detection and evaluation reliability is currently under review
